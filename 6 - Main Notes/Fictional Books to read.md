If you mean **fantasy worlds where the characters are intelligent, the story is a mystery/thriller, and the plot constantly makes you rethink what you know**, these are excellent:

### Best matches

1. **The Stormlight Archive — Brandon Sanderson**
    
    - Massive fantasy world
        
    - Extremely clever magic system
        
    - Political schemes, hidden identities, ancient secrets
        
    - Lots of “wait, WHAT?” reveals
        
    - **Smart-character factor:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Plot twists:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Worldbuilding:** ★★★★★
        
2. **The First Law — Joe Abercrombie**
    
    - Dark, cynical fantasy
        
    - Characters are intelligent, manipulative and morally complicated
        
    - Political maneuvering and betrayals
        
    - Twists often come from realizing characters aren't what they seemed
        
    - **Smart-character factor:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Plot twists:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Darkness:** ★★★★★
        
3. **Mistborn — Brandon Sanderson**
    
    - One of the best choices if you want **fantasy + heist + mystery + twists**
        
    - Characters constantly figure out how to exploit the rules of the world
        
    - The magic system itself becomes part of the mystery
        
    - The ending of the first trilogy is particularly satisfying.
        
    - **Smart-character factor:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Plot twists:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Accessibility:** ★★★★★
        
4. **The Lies of Locke Lamora — Scott Lynch**
    
    - Basically **fantasy Ocean's Eleven**
        
    - Criminal masterminds, elaborate schemes, deception and counter-deception
        
    - Extremely entertaining dialogue
        
    - More focused on clever characters than epic worldbuilding.
        
    - **Smart-character factor:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Schemes:** ★★★★★
        
    - **Thriller:** ★★★★☆
        
5. **The Poppy War — R.F. Kuang**
    
    - Starts as military/academic fantasy and becomes considerably darker
        
    - Strategy, politics, warfare and mythology
        
    - Characters make increasingly consequential decisions.
        
    - **Smart-character factor:** ★★★★☆
        
    - **Plot twists:** ★★★★☆
        
    - **Darkness:** ★★★★★
        

### If you want something REALLY twisty

**The Will of the Many — James Islington**

This is probably one of my strongest recommendations for your description.

It's a fantasy mystery involving:

- an elite academy
    
- political conspiracies
    
- secret societies
    
- an intelligent protagonist
    
- ancient mysteries
    
- competing factions
    
- progressively larger revelations
    

It has that **“I need to know what the hell is actually going on”** feeling.

**Smart characters:** ★★★★★  
**Mystery:** ★★★★★  
**Twists:** ★★★★★  
**Worldbuilding:** ★★★★★

- manipulation

### If you want anime/manga

**Attack on Titan** — probably the strongest match.

It begins as survival/action fantasy but gradually turns into an enormous mystery. Information is deliberately withheld from you, and later revelations can completely change how you interpret earlier events.

**Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood** is another excellent choice if you want:

- intelligent characters
    
- conspiracy
    
- politics
    
- mystery
    
- carefully constructed foreshadowing
    
- satisfying payoffs.
    

And **Hunter × Hunter** if your priority is **characters outsmarting one another**. Its battles frequently feel like psychological chess rather than power contests.
