YouTube clipping can pay very well, but only if you're treating it as a media business rather than simply reposting clips. Most channels never earn meaningful income, while the top ones generate thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars per month.

### How much can clipping channels make?

There are two main models:

|Channel size|Monthly views|Estimated monthly revenue*|
|---|--:|--:|
|Small|100k–500k|$100–$2,000|
|Growing|1M–10M|$1,000–$30,000|
|Large|10M–100M+|$10,000–$300,000+|

*Assuming the content is monetized and mostly long-form. Shorts typically earn much less per view.

Keep in mind:

- **Long-form videos** generally have much higher ad revenue (RPM often $2–$15+, depending on niche).
    
- **Shorts** often earn only a fraction of that (sometimes around $0.02–$0.20 per 1,000 views, though it varies widely).
    

Many successful clipping channels also earn through:

- Affiliate marketing
    
- Sponsorships
    
- Selling courses or communities
    
- Newsletter traffic
    

These often exceed YouTube ad revenue.

## The most successful clipping niches

### 1. Podcasts (One of the best)

Examples:

- Business podcasts
    
- Entrepreneurship
    
- Self-improvement
    
- Comedy podcasts
    

Pros:

- Huge amount of content
    
- Strong audience retention
    
- High CPM if business-focused
    

### 2. Finance & Investing

Examples:

- Warren Buffett clips
    
- Investing interviews
    
- Market analysis
    

Pros:

- Among the highest advertising rates.
    
- Audience is valuable to advertisers.
    

### 3. Business & Entrepreneurship

Examples:

- CEO interviews
    
- Startup founders
    
- Productivity advice
    

High CPM and evergreen content.

### 4. Education

Examples:

- Science
    
- Psychology
    
- History
    
- Economics
    

Evergreen with long-term search traffic.

### 5. Gaming Highlights

Examples:

- Streamer moments
    
- Tournament highlights
    
- Funny gameplay
    

Can generate enormous views but usually has lower RPM than finance or business.

### 6. Sports

Examples:

- Analysis
    
- Reactions
    
- Historical moments
    

Avoid uploading copyrighted game footage without permission. Commentary and analysis are generally safer than simply reposting broadcasts.

### 7. AI & Tech

Examples:

- AI news
    
- Product launches
    
- Programming tips
    
- Tech interviews
    

Growing rapidly and tends to attract higher-value advertisers.

### 8. Motivation & Self-Improvement

Examples:

- Speeches
    
- Success stories
    
- Productivity clips
    

Highly shareable but competitive.

## What performs best today?

Generally, the strongest combination of view potential and monetization is:

1. Business podcasts
    
2. Finance/investing
    
3. AI & technology
    
4. Education
    
5. Productivity
    
6. Comedy podcasts
    
7. Gaming
    
8. Celebrity interviews
    

## The biggest risk: Copyright

Many people assume clipping is "free money," but simply reuploading someone else's content can lead to:

- Copyright claims (the original creator receives the ad revenue)
    
- Copyright strikes
    
- Demonetization for reused content
    
- Channel removal in serious or repeated cases
    

Channels that succeed usually add substantial original value by:

- Editing for pacing
    
- Adding captions
    
- Including commentary or analysis
    
- Rearranging content into a new narrative
    
- Creating original thumbnails and titles
    

## If you're starting today

A relatively strong approach would be:

- Pick one niche (AI, business, or finance tend to monetize well).
    
- Focus on long-form videos (8–15 minutes) rather than only Shorts.
    
- Use Shorts to drive viewers to long-form content.
    
- Publish consistently (for example, 1–2 long videos and several Shorts each week).
    
- Ensure each video is meaningfully transformed rather than just copied to improve both monetization prospects and copyright compliance.
    

If executed well with original, transformative editing and a monetizable niche, a clipping channel can realistically grow into a four- or five-figure monthly business, but it is not guaranteed and competition is intense.
